# Brand: Stabrix

**Liquidity that never leaves.** Parchment and ink: a nautical chart, hand-inked cards, serif numbers. Light only. Tokens live in `app/src/app/globals.css`; the landing has its own scoped sheet in `app/src/app/saltwake.css`.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #e6e0cf | page background, inputs |
| surface | #ece7d8 | cards |
| surface-hover | #e0d8c3 | hover, selected rows |
| surface-line | #16130e | ink borders and card shadows |
| line | #b7ad95 | dashed rules |
| ink | #201c15 | text, primary buttons |
| muted | #6b6250 | secondary text |
| on-accent | #f3ecd8 | text on ink buttons |
| up | #2f7d47 | live, gains |
| down | #b4432f | halted, losses, crash actions |

Coin tones: USDC steel #9fb3cf, USDT sage #8fbf9a, DAI sand #e9c46a, USDS scan #7fc8c2, USDe rose #e59a8c, EURC iris #b8c0f0, BRZ leaf #a3d17a, IDRX amber #f0b36a. Range presets: Tight signal #d9f27a, Balanced scan, Wide sand. Backstop: gold #f6c945.

## Typography

- Titles: Goudy Bookletter 1911, uppercase.
- Numbers and prose: EB Garamond with lining, tabular figures (old-style digits turn "1inch" into "ıinch").
- UI text and labels: Open Sans; labels 10.5px bold uppercase with 0.14em tracking and a diamond mark.
- Buttons: Josefin Sans, uppercase pills.
- Addresses and code: JetBrains Mono.

## Shape

Cards: 3px ink border, 26px radius, offset ink shadow. Inner fields: 2px ink border, 16 to 20px radius. Buttons and selectors fully rounded. Rules are 2px dashed.

## Voice

Short, specific, active. Numbers over adjectives. No em dashes.
